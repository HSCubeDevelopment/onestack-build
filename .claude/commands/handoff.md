Write an end-of-day handoff note.

Ask if this handoff is from **Sidh** or **Harjaap** and confirm today's date. Then create an entry in
the Notion **Handoff Log** with a one-line **Summary**, **Who**, **Date**, and a body of:

- **What I did**
- **Where I stopped**
- **What's next**
- **Blocked / watch out for**

Fill it from this session. Keep it short.
